Privacy & Your Data

This page was built to be respectful with your information from the ground up.

What gets processed

Your birth date, time, name (optional), gender, and timezone. All calculation happens in your browser using JavaScript. None of these inputs are transmitted to any server, stored in any database, or logged anywhere.

No cookies, no tracking

This site does not set any cookies. There is no analytics tracking, no advertising pixel, no fingerprinting, and no session persistence. Close the tab and your reading is gone unless you saved the image manually.

Third-party resources we load

To render properly, your browser loads fonts and one helper library from external CDNs:

  • Google Fonts (Cormorant Garamond, DM Mono, Noto Serif SC) — Google may receive your IP address and User-Agent when fonts are loaded. We use only fonts, no Google Analytics or tracking.
  • cdnjs.cloudflare.com — loads the html2canvas library used when you click "Share this" to generate your soul signature image. Cloudflare may receive your IP for this request.

If you join the optional waitlist below, your email goes to a private Substack list managed under Substack's privacy policy. Nothing else.

Legal basis

Under GDPR, processing your birth details in your own browser to generate a reading you requested is performed on the basis of your direct consent (Art. 6(1)(a)), given by filling in the form. Since no data leaves your device, the question of cross-border transfer doesn't arise.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, or erase any data we hold about you. Since we don't hold any, this is automatic for the calculator. For the waitlist, write to hello@shamathakur.carrd.co and your email will be removed.

Operator

This project is run as a personal creative endeavour by Shama Thakur, Stockholm, Sweden. Not a registered company. For questions, reach out via my site.
